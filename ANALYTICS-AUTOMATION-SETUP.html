Automating the monthly read

I can't reach Google Analytics directly — there's no connector for it. But Google Drive is connected, so a spreadsheet in the middle bridges the gap. You set it up once; after that the data arrives on its own and I do the reading.

One-time setup: ~25 min Then: 2 cell edits a month GA4 G-VCL4K7HGYJ

The pipeline

Do step 9 of the setup guide first. The reports below pull customEvent:click_action and friends. Those only exist if you've registered them as custom dimensions in GA4. Skip that and four of the six reports come back full of (not set) — and GA4 does not backfill, so the month is lost.

Step 1 — Create the sheet and install the add-on

1

Google Sheets → new spreadsheet

~5 minutes

Name it exactly — I'll look it up by name, so a typo means I can't find it:

Cookaki GA4 Monthly Data

Leave it in My Drive on the same Google account the Drive connector uses. Then:

Sheets → Extensions → Add-ons → Get add-ons → search GA4 Reports Builder for Google Analytics → Install

It's Google's own add-on, free. Authorise it against the account that has access to G-VCL4K7HGYJ.

There are several similarly named third-party add-ons in the Marketplace. The official one is published by Google and titled GA4 Reports Builder for Google Analytics™.

Step 3 — Date range, and the one honest compromise

3

Calendar months vs full automation — pick one

~2 minutes

OptionDate rangeTrade-off
Calendar month
recommended		 explicit dates, e.g.
2026-07-01 → 2026-07-31		 Clean months that match how you think about the business and compare year on year. Costs you two cell edits on the config sheet each month.
Rolling window 30daysAgo → yesterday Zero maintenance, genuinely hands-off. But months overlap and vary in length, so year-on-year comparisons get muddy and February looks artificially strong.
For a seasonal restaurant I'd take the calendar month and the two edits. Athens in August and Athens in February are different businesses, and you'll want clean year-on-year numbers by next summer. Two cells is a small price.

Step 4 — Schedule it

4

Monthly refresh

~1 minute

Extensions → GA4 Reports Builder → Schedule reports → Monthly

The add-on then re-runs all six reports on its own and overwrites the tabs with fresh figures.

Then tell me the sheet is live and I'll set up a scheduled task for the 3rd of each month: read the six tabs, compare against every prior month in the tracker, append the new row, and write you a short read — what moved, the likeliest explanation, and what deserves a look. A few days' gap after month end lets GA4 finish processing.

Three limitations worth knowing up front. The task can only see what's in the sheet — a new question later means editing a report config first. If someone renames the sheet or a tab, the task fails until it's pointed at the new name. And an automated summary is only as honest as the data underneath: it inherits the consent gap, so it will still be describing the visitors who accepted cookies, not all of them.
