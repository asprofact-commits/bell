How these were counted

  • Source. Cookaki_Reviews_reviews_2026-07-22.csv — 477 reviews. The headline counts use the 249 reviews published after 31 March 2026, of which 221 contain written text; all-time counts use all 477.
  • Method. Every superlative ("best", "favourite", "#1", "miglior", "mejor", "meilleur", "καλύτερο", and equivalents in 17 languages, accent-normalised) was located and read in context. A review counts only when the superlative is explicitly tied to Athens, Greece or Europe.
  • One review, one count. Where a guest claims more than one geography, the review is counted at its widest — so no review appears twice in the totals.
  • Venue-level vs food-level. Venue-level means the restaurant or the place is named as best. Food-level means the food, meal or dish is named as best. Both are shown separately because only the first is a competitive ranking of the business itself.
  • Every quote above is verbatim. Non-English quotes are shown in the original with a translation beneath.
