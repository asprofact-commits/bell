The shape of it

◆

One Drive folder, one naming rule

Set up once · everything downstream depends on it

Rather than pasting figures into tabs by hand, drop the raw exports into Drive and let me parse them. Create this folder in the same Google account the Drive connector uses:

My Drive / Cookaki Analytics /

Each month, save the downloads into it using this exact pattern — platform, then year and month:

FileFrom
gbp-2026-07.csvGoogle Business Profile
instagram-2026-07.csvMeta Business Suite
tiktok-2026-07.csvTikTok Studio

GA4 keeps writing to its own sheet, Cookaki GA4 Monthly Data, automatically. TripAdvisor has no file — those four numbers get typed straight into the tracker.

Why filenames and not tabs. Downloading and dropping takes seconds and can't go wrong. Copy-pasting columns into a spreadsheet is where transcription errors creep in, and a wrong number that looks plausible is worse than a missing one. Let the machine read the machine's file.

Channel by channel

Google Business Profile

DOWNLOAD Probably your largest channel — and the one most people never measure
Why firstMost people looking for a Koukaki restaurant never reach your website. They search Maps, look at the photos, and tap directions. Your GA4 directions_click only sees the small minority who came via the site.
Search "my business" while signed in, or go to business.google.com → your profile → Performance
  1. Set the date range to the previous calendar month.
  2. Read the overview: searches that showed your profile split into discovery and direct, plus profile views.
  3. Open the interactions breakdown for direction requests, calls and website clicks.
  4. Use Download report to get the CSV, and save it as gbp-2026-07.csv.
FeedsTracker tab Google Business — columns B through H
WatchDiscovery searches. Those are people who did not search for you by name. That number is your actual reach into new demand, and it's the one that responds to photos, review volume and category accuracy.
A Performance API exists if you ever want this fully automated, but it needs a Google Cloud project and OAuth setup — disproportionate for one location. Three minutes a month is cheaper than maintaining a developer integration.

Instagram

DOWNLOAD Requires a Business or Creator account
business.facebook.com → Insights → set date range to last full month
  1. Results gives the account-level figures: reach, accounts engaged, profile visits, website taps.
  2. Content gives post-level performance. Use the Export Data button (top right, near the date filter) for CSV or XLS.
  3. Save as instagram-2026-07.csv in the Drive folder.
  4. Note your follower count at month end — it isn't in the export.
FeedsTracker tab Instagram — columns B, D, E, F, G, H
WatchWebsite taps against Referral sessions on the Monthly Log tab. If Instagram reports 300 taps and GA4 shows 30 referral sessions, something is losing nine out of ten people — usually the in-app browser, or a link that 404s.
~90 day retention. Miss a quarter and those months are unrecoverable. If you only automate one reminder, make it this one.

TikTok

DOWNLOAD Shortest memory of the four
TikTok Studio → Analytics → date range → Download data · or business.tiktok.com for up to a year of history
  1. Set the range to the previous calendar month.
  2. Download the Overview category — views, profile views, likes, comments, shares, followers.
  3. Only one category exports at a time. Overview is enough for the tracker; grab Content too if you want per-video detail.
  4. Save as tiktok-2026-07.csv.
FeedsTracker tab TikTok — columns B, D, E, F, G, H, I
Honest noteThis is the thinnest of the four in business terms. TikTok views tell you about content, not covers. Your site already counts outbound TikTok clicks as social_tiktok, and inbound traffic lands in Referral. Track it if you're investing in it; skip it without guilt if you aren't.
60 days in Studio. Use business.tiktok.com if you ever need to reach further back — it holds about a year.

TripAdvisor

MANUAL ONLY And no honest way around it
WhyThere is no owner analytics API. The Content API is for displaying reviews on your own site and requires approval. Scraping the Management Center breaks their terms of service, so it's off the table.
Management Center → your listing → Reviews and Analytics
  1. Total review count.
  2. Average rating.
  3. Rank among Athens restaurants.
  4. How many of this month's reviews you replied to.
FeedsTracker tab TripAdvisor — columns B, D, E, F, H. New reviews and rank change calculate themselves from the month before.
WatchReply rate. It's the only one of these four you fully control, and the tracker computes it for you. Ranking moves slowly; replies are the lever.
