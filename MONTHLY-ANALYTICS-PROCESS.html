The monthly 30 minutes

Once the container is live, GA4 will answer about a dozen questions worth acting on. This is the fixed route through them — same order, same numbers, first week of every month.

First month: +40 min one-time setup Every month after: ~30 min Companion: Cookaki-Monthly-Analytics-Tracker.xlsx

First, the honest boundaries

What this setup genuinely knows

  • How many people found you, and through which channel
  • Which country and city they're in — tourists vs locals
  • Which of the six language versions they read
  • Every online booking, with party size, date and time slot
  • Every tap on WhatsApp, phone, directions, Order Online
  • Where on the page that tap happened
  • Which SEO landing pages actually lead to bookings

What it will never know

  • Visitors who declined cookies — invisible, entirely
  • Walk-ins, and phone bookings made after a tel: tap
  • Whether a booking was honoured or a no-show
  • Revenue, covers, or average spend
  • Bookings taken over WhatsApp by hand
  • Anyone who found you on Google Maps and never touched the site
Read every number as a trend, not a count. Consent Mode means GA4 sees only the visitors who accepted the banner. If that's 60% of them, every figure below is roughly 60% of reality — but consistently so. "Directions clicks rose 30% this month" is trustworthy. "We had exactly altogether 214 directions clicks" is not.

Fix this before your first month

!

Turn off Form interactions in Enhanced Measurement

2 minutes · otherwise your form numbers will be wrong from day one

GA4's Enhanced Measurement has a Form interactions toggle that automatically emits an event called — unfortunately — form_submit. That is the exact name your GTM tag uses. Left on, the two merge into one inflated event, and you can't tell them apart afterwards.

Your GTM-SETUP.md says Enhanced Measurement "will not collide" with the custom events. That's right for clicks — GA4's automatic event is click, yours is ui_click. It is not right for forms. This is the one exception.
GA4 → Admin → Data collection and modification → Data streams → your web stream → Enhanced measurement (gear icon)
Enhanced measurement

Automatically measure interactions and content on your site.

ScrollsON
Outbound clicksON

Video engagementON
File downloadsON
Form interactions 1TURN OFF
  • 1
    Switch it off. Your forms hand off to WhatsApp via JavaScript, which GA4's automatic form tracking misreads anyway — you lose nothing and keep a clean form_submit.
Leave Outbound clicks on. It produces click, not ui_click, so there's no collision — and it quietly catches any external link you add in future before you've thought about tagging it.

The monthly route — seven stops

First week of the month. Set the date range to last full calendar month and switch on Add comparison → Preceding period before you start; every stop then shows change as well as level. Numbers marked LOG go into the tracker.

1

Sanity check 2 min

Reports → Engagement → Events

Scan the event list. You are looking for eight familiar names and nothing weird: page_view, ui_click, reservation_complete, form_submit, whatsapp_click, order_online_click, directions_click, phone_click.

If ui_click is missing or has collapsed to near zero, stop the review and check the container — a site deploy has probably overwritten something. Analysing a broken month wastes the hour twice.
2

How many, and from where 5 min

Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition

Read the session totals by default channel group: Organic Search, Direct, Referral, Organic Social.

Log Users · Sessions · New users % · Engagement rate · sessions from Organic Search, Direct, Referral, Organic Social
The decision this drives Organic Search rising month over month means the SEO landing pages are working and deserve more. Direct rising usually means offline — signage, word of mouth, the plaque outside. Referral spikes are worth chasing to their source: someone wrote about you.
3

Tourists or locals 4 min

Reports → User → User attributes → Demographic details → Country, then City

Greece versus everywhere else, then Athens as a share of Greece.

Log % of sessions from Greece · top non-Greek country
The decision this drives The whole positioning of the site — "loved by locals, not a tourist trap" — depends on the ratio. It also tells you which language mirror to invest in next, and it's seasonal: watch it move between August and February rather than reading one month in isolation.
4

What they did 6 min

Reports → Engagement → Key events

The five actions that mean something, plus form submissions.

EventWhat it really means
reservation_completeA confirmed table. Your one true conversion.
whatsapp_clickOpened a conversation — often a booking you took by hand
order_online_clickSent to the delivery platform
directions_clickSomeone standing nearby deciding to walk over. High intent.

Log all six counts. The tracker computes total contact actions, contact rate and booking rate for you.
The decision this drives Booking rate is the number to defend. If sessions climb while bookings stay flat, you have a traffic win and a conversion problem — go to stop 5. If directions and phone dominate bookings, your visitors are nearby and impulsive, which argues for the sticky bar and against long-form persuasion.
5

Which CTA won 5 min

Explore → CTA performance (built above) → set date range

Read the action × placement table. Then add Page language as a third row for one minute, to check nothing is broken in a mirror — a translated page with zero clicks on a CTA that works elsewhere usually means a broken link, not disinterest.

Log best-performing placement · its share of all clicks
6

Which pages pulled them in 5 min

Reports → Engagement → Landing page

Sort by sessions, then look at the Key events column beside each. Your SEO pages — greek-restaurant-near-acropolis, where-to-eat-in-koukaki, vegetarian-greek-koukaki — and the dish pages are the ones to watch.

Log top landing page and its sessions · best-converting landing page
The decision this drives A page with high sessions and no key events is attracting the wrong reader, or failing to ask. A page with few sessions but a high conversion rate deserves promotion and internal links — that's your next piece of writing, told to you by the data.
7

Booking shape 3 min

Explore → Booking detail (built above)

Log average party size · busiest slot · quietest slot
The decision this drives Slot design and staffing, and whether the last seatings are worth keeping.

What Tag Manager gives you monthly

⚙

Nothing, and that's correct

GTM is plumbing, not reporting — but it needs one habit

Tag Manager has no reports. It has no numbers. Everything you measure lives in GA4. What GTM needs from you is a five-minute check, and only after something changes:

WhenDo thisWhy
After any site deployRun Preview, click one CTA, confirm ui_click still firesA changed class name or restructured section can silently break placement detection
After any container editPreview, then Submit with a descriptive version nameVersions becomes a readable history — and one-click rollback when something breaks
QuarterlySkim Tags for anything not firingOrphaned tags accumulate and confuse whoever inherits this
QuarterlyRe-run the console snippet in GTM-SETUP-GUIDE.htmlCatches double-counting introduced by a well-meaning plugin

Reading the movements

What you seeUsually meansWhat to check next
Sessions up, bookings flatWrong traffic, or a conversion problemLanding page report — is the new traffic hitting a page that never asks for the booking?
Bookings up, sessions flatSomething on the site got betterCompare CTA placements against last month; whatever you changed, keep it
Everything down togetherSeasonality, or measurement brokeStop 1 first, always. Then compare with the same month last year, not last month
