Getting here

From the Acropolis Museum

5 minutes on foot. Head south down Makrigiánni, cross Syngroú-Fix, take Petméza on your left. The bell is on the right at no. 5.

By metro

Syngroú-Fix station (Red Line, M2) — 4 minutes walking. Akrópoli station (M2) — 8 minutes. Both lines connect to Athens International Airport via Syntagma.

From central Athens

From Sýntagma 10 minutes by taxi or 15 minutes on foot via Dionysíou Areopagítou — the pedestrian street that circles the Acropolis.

From the airport

X95 express bus to Sýntagma (~60 min, €5.50), then metro M2 one stop to Syngroú-Fix. Or taxi (~€40 flat rate, 40 min).

Parking

Street parking on neighbouring blocks (paid 09:00–21:00). Closest paid garage: Syngroú-Fix area, 4 minutes on foot.

Hotels nearby

Cookaki is nestled in a strategic, relaxed spot at Petmeza 5, Koukáki — just a swift 3-minute taxi ride or a brief 10-minute stroll from major Syngrou Avenue landmarks, including the Grand Hyatt Athens and the Athenaeum InterContinental. Ideal for hotel guests and families seeking an authentic, smart-casual seafood destination with tailored table-side fish selection. We also deliver to and welcome guests from all Koukáki, Makrigiánni and Acropolis-area hotels.
