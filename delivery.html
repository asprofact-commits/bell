How it works

  1. Choose how to order. Order online for one-tap delivery. WhatsApp for special requests, large orders or pickup.
  2. Choose your dishes. The full menu is online — Moussaka, Pastítsio, Beef Kokkinistó, daily specials, desserts, drinks.
  3. We pack hot. Heat-sealed containers, sauces separate when needed, bread wrapped warm.
  4. You eat. 25–45 minutes typical. Hotel concierge or apartment door — we deliver everywhere central Athens.

Delivery questions

Where does COOKaki deliver?

Across central Athens — all hotels in Koukáki, Makrigiánni, Acropolis, Pláka and Thiseío. Live coverage is shown when you enter your address.

What's the minimum order?

Typically €10 when ordering online. WhatsApp orders have no minimum, but pickup is encouraged for smaller orders to keep things hot.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 25–45 minutes. Friday and Saturday nights run a little longer — the app shows a live estimate.

Can I order for a specific time?

Yes — you can schedule when you order online. For more specific timing (a hotel arrival, a birthday) WhatsApp us at +30 698 237 3505.

Can I order for a large group?

For 6+ portions or set menus, WhatsApp us. We'll quote, prep ahead and coordinate the delivery — no app required.

Can I pick up myself?

Yes — message us on WhatsApp or call +30 21 0921 9818. We'll have it ready at the bell.

Do you deliver to hotels?

Constantly — we know most of the Koukáki and Makrigiánni hotels by name. Just enter the hotel address when you order online, or tell us on WhatsApp.

Hungry now?

Online ordering is open during all our service hours — and so are we.
