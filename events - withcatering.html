Catering Across Athens

When you can't come to us — we come to you.

Office terraces, garden weddings, christenings at home, corporate launches, baby showers — wherever the celebration is, we pack the kitchen, plate on site, and leave the room cleaner than we found it.

What we bring

  1. The food, packed hot — heat-sealed insulated containers, sauces separate, breads warm.
  2. The plating team — for groups of 25+, our team plates on site, in your kitchen or terrace, so it looks like dinner just came off the pass.
  3. The service equipment — chafing dishes, serving platters, linen, sometimes a small table setup.
  4. The clean-up — we take it all away. You return to your home/office/terrace exactly as you left it.

Catering styles

  • Drop-off catering — €18–€25/pp. We deliver hot, plated on platters, you serve yourself.
  • Plated dinner service — €30–€45/pp. Our team plates and serves at the table.
  • Buffet / family-style — €25–€35/pp. Long tables, sharing plates, Greek-style abundance.
  • Cocktail receptions — €20–€35/pp. Small plates, mezze, hand-passed by our team.
  • Full event production — POA. Wedding-scale events, multi-day, multi-meal.

All prices are starting points. Final pricing depends on menu, group size, distance, and service style. We quote in writing within 24 hours.

Event & catering questions

How many guests can you seat for a private event?

Our Koukáki dining room comfortably seats 12 to 50. For groups of 50+, we coordinate full buy-outs with dedicated seating plans and team. For under 12, you don't need a private event — just reserve a table.

What do private events cost?

In-venue events: set menus from €18/person (children's birthday) to €45/person (full buy-out, three courses, premium wines). Catering: from €18/person (drop-off) to €55/person (full plated service). Final pricing depends on menu, group, and service style — we quote in writing within 24 hours.

Do you require a deposit?

For private events and catering we typically request a 10–20% deposit to confirm the date. Refundable up to 7 days before the event; applied to your final bill.

How far in advance do I need to book?

For weekday events: 1–2 weeks. For weekends and full buy-outs: 3–6 weeks recommended. For wedding-season dates (May–September): 2–3 months. Last-minute inquiries — WhatsApp us, we sometimes have openings.

Can you accommodate dietary needs (vegan, gluten-free, allergies)?

Yes — always. Tell us when you inquire and we'll build the menu around it. Our regular menu already includes clearly tagged vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options.

Do you cater outside central Athens?

We regularly cater across central Athens — Koukáki, Pláka, Sýntagma, Kolonáki, Glyfáda. For further locations (north suburbs, coast), there's a small delivery fee and we'll confirm logistics. Athens islands (Aígina, Spétses, Pórous) — possible for larger events, ask us.

Can you provide drinks and wine?

Yes — full beverage service, with wines from small Greek estates we curate ourselves. We can also work with your own bring-your-own (no corkage fee for events). For weddings, we coordinate with your bar service.

Do you supply staff for catering events?

For events with plated service or 25+ guests, our team comes with the food: a kitchen lead, plating staff, and service staff. Drop-off catering is just us, the food, and you.
