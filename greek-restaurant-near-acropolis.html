Near the Acropolis

A Greek restaurant
five minutes from the Acropolis Museum.

COOKaki sits at Petmeza 5 in Koukáki — two quiet blocks south of the Acropolis Museum. Honest, homemade Greek cooking since 1956, at neighbourhood prices, on a street where Athenians still outnumber tourists.

Distance & directions

How to walk here from the Acropolis

From the Acropolis Museum, walk south down Makrygiánni and cross into Koukáki — Petmeza 5 is about five minutes on foot, two blocks from the museum's southern side. From the Acropolis entrance on Dionysíou Areopagítou, it is roughly a ten-minute downhill stroll through the pedestrian streets. Pláka and Monastiráki are about fifteen minutes away.

The nearest metro is Akrópoli (Line 2, red), a three-minute walk from our door — convenient if you are coming from Sýntagma, Monastiráki or the airport line via Sýntagma.

  • Acropolis Museum — 5 min walk (≈400 m)
  • Acropolis / Parthenon — 10 min walk
  • Akrópoli metro (Line 2) — 3 min walk
  • Pláka & Monastiráki — 15 min walk
  • Filopáppou Hill — 8 min walk
Why locals eat here

The neighbourhood table, not a tourist trap.

🏛️

Genuinely close, genuinely local

Most restaurants this near the Acropolis are built for one-time visitors. COOKaki has fed the same Koukáki streets since 1956 — locals come back weekly, which is the surest sign the food is honest.

💶

Fair prices beneath the rock

Mains are €8–€13 and a full meal runs about €15–€20 a head with wine — a rarity within a five-minute walk of the museum, where prices usually climb with the view.

🌿

Light enough to keep sightseeing

We cook with Greek olive oil, fresh vegetables and lighter techniques — a proper Greek lunch that lets you walk back up to the Parthenon, not nap in the park.

What to order

If it's your first time

Start with the Traditional Moussaka (€9.90), the dish the kitchen has been known for since 1956. Add the daily fresh fish from the Evia Gulf (€12.90), a Greek salad, and the Garden Dish of the Day (€8.90) if you want something vegetable-forward. Finish with the San Sebastián burnt cheesecake. Ask the team what came out of the oven that hour — the daily specials are where the kitchen has the most fun.

FAQ

Eating near the Acropolis

How far is COOKaki from the Acropolis Museum?
About a five-minute walk. We're at Petmeza 5, two blocks south of the museum. The Acropolis entrance is roughly ten minutes on foot; Pláka and Monastiráki about fifteen.
Is this a tourist restaurant or a local one?
A genuine neighbourhood taverna that has cooked for Koukáki since 1956 — the kind of place Athenians bring their own families to. 4.8 ★ on Google across 370+ reviews, 4.9 ★ on Tripadvisor.
Do I need to book?
Walk-ins are always welcome, but in high season (April–October) and on weekends we strongly recommend reserving. Book on this site or WhatsApp us at +30 698 237 3505.
What does a meal cost?
Mains are €8–€13; expect about €15–€20 per person for a full meal with a glass of wine — excellent value this close to the Acropolis.
Can I get delivery or takeaway to my hotel nearby?
Yes — order online or WhatsApp the kitchen. We deliver to the Koukáki and Makrygiánni hotels around the Acropolis Museum constantly.
