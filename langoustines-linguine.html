Signature Dish

Langoustines & artisan linguine
the Aegean, on one plate.

Cookaki's Langoustines with Artisan Linguine (€19.90) are sweet Aegean langoustines laid over hand-cut linguine in a light shellfish sauce, finished with Greek extra-virgin olive oil. Our signature seafood plate — five minutes from the Acropolis Museum.

What makes it

Sweet shellfish, restrained sauce

This is seafood cooked the Greek way — let the ingredient lead, and stay out of its way.

  • Langoustines — sweet, delicate Aegean shellfish, cooked just to set
  • Artisan linguine — hand-cut pasta with real bite
  • Light shellfish sauce — built from the langoustine heads, not cream
  • Greek extra-virgin olive oil — the finishing gloss
  • A squeeze of lemon — the only seasoning it asks for

€19.90 · the plate to order when you want the sea to be the whole story.
