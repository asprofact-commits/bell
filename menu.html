The Bell Journey

Feel Greece
beyond the obvious.

We don't hand you a list of dishes. We hand you a journey. Begin with a Main Act, then compose it your way — or let our Chef compose it for you. Prices include VAT. Allergens on request.

Choose Your Main Act ↓ Reserve & Begin the Journey
How the journey works

Four moves. One evening you'll remember.

  1. 01

    Choose your Main Act

    The anchor of your table. Three Greeces, three feelings — comfort, sea, or fire. Everything else is composed around it.

  2. 02

    Complete it with Essentials

    Bread from a 1907 Athenian oven. Water from the springs. The Yiayia's Garden. The quiet things that make a table whole.

  3. 03

    Deepen it with a Trilogy

    Three tastes at a time — from the family table, the fisherman's coast, the open flame, or the Art of Time.

  4. 04

    Or let the Chef compose it

    A Symphony is our Chef's own harmony around your Main Act. One decision. The whole journey, arranged.

Bell Essentials, the Liquid Sail and the Trilogies are designed to complete your journey — they are offered exclusively alongside a Main Act.

The Fisherman's Catch of the DayChef-composed

The Fish & The Nest · Coastal — €24.80 · Deep-sea fish · Chef-filleted — €29.70

Our Chef selects the best whole fish from today's coastal and deep-sea catch, then carefully prepares and fillets it for you. Simply cooked to preserve the character of the fish, accompanied by a harmony of organic seasonal vegetables and wild greens, finished with "Harmonia" from the award-winning Sakellaropoulos extra virgin olive oil collection — because when the catch is this fresh, the sea needs very little else.
