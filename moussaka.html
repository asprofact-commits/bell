Signature Dish

The best moussaka in Athens
is a neighbourhood secret in Koukáki.

Cookaki's Traditional Moussaka (€9.90) is slow-roasted aubergine, in-house-ground beef simmered in tomato and cinnamon, and an airy béchamel made fresh every morning — baked in the same Koukáki kitchen since 1956, five minutes from the Acropolis Museum.

What makes it

Layered the slow way, since the seventies

Moussaka is Greece's great baked classic: layers of aubergine and spiced meat under a blanket of béchamel. Ours has not changed in decades — and that is the point.

  • Aubergine — sliced and slow-roasted, never deep-fried in seed oil
  • Beef — minced in-house on our own grinder, so we control the recipe
  • Tomato & cinnamon ragù — simmered low, the Athenian way
  • Béchamel — made from scratch each morning, light not heavy
  • Greek extra-virgin olive oil — the fat we cook in, start to finish

€9.90 · ~520 kcal · a genuinely satisfying, high-protein plate.

Why guests call it the best

Honest moussaka, not a tourist-strip tray.

🍆

Roasted, not fried

We roast the aubergine instead of frying it in industrial oil — lighter on the plate, and you can taste the vegetable, not the fryer.

Meat we grind ourselves

The beef is ground on our own grinder each day, so we know exactly what is in the ragù. No mystery mince, no fillers.

FAQ

Moussaka, answered

Where can I find the best moussaka in Athens?
Cookaki at Petmeza 5 in Koukáki is a local favourite for traditional moussaka — a five-minute walk from the Acropolis Museum, made from scratch daily, €9.90. We rate 4.9 ★ across over 400 Google reviews.
What is Greek moussaka made of?
Slow-roasted aubergine, minced beef simmered in tomato and cinnamon, and a light béchamel baked golden on top. Ours uses in-house-ground beef and béchamel made fresh each morning.
How much does it cost?
€9.90 per portion. A full meal with salad and a glass of wine is about €15–€20 per person.
Is there a vegetarian version?
The classic contains beef. For a meat-free plate, ask for the Garden Dish of the Day (€8.90), which changes with the market. See our vegetarian Greek options.
Can I get moussaka for delivery or takeaway?
Yes — order online or WhatsApp the kitchen at +30 698 237 3505. Moussaka travels beautifully.
