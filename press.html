Cookaki
in the press.

Where writers and editors have written about the kitchen on Petmeza 5. We keep it short here and link out — read each piece in full where it was published.

Athens Voice · Feature · 24 August 2026 · Konstantina Voulgareli

Cookaki: Το μικρό γαστρονομικό μυστικό στη σκιά της Ακρόπολης που αξίζει να ανακαλύψεις αυτό το καλοκαίρι

“Cookaki: the small gastronomic secret in the shadow of the Acropolis worth discovering this summer”

In August 2026, Athens Voice came to Petmeza 5 and wrote about the room and the food. The piece follows the history of the address — a kitchen that has fed this neighbourhood since 1956, when it was the wine-taverna Kambana, whose regulars included the poet Yiannis Ritsos — and connects that history to the way we cook now.

It describes the philosophy we work by: honest cooking, seasonality, Greek produce chosen each morning at the farmers’ market, the fish market or Varvakios by our chef, Marios Toutountzoglou. And it stops at the dish we are proudest of — a round courgette stuffed with sour trahana and wild fish, finished with avgolemono.

We don’t reproduce the article here. It belongs to Athens Voice, and it reads better where it was published.
