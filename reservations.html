Good to know

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes — we always keep a few tables free for walk-ins. On Friday and Saturday nights we strongly recommend reserving ahead.

Can I really order my plates in advance?

Yes. Our hot table changes daily — when you pick a day, you'll see exactly what's cooking and can reserve your plates so they're set aside for your table. You pay at the restaurant as usual.

How big a group can you seat?

Up to 8 guests through the form. For 9 or more, WhatsApp us at +30 698 237 3505 — we'll arrange a table and, if you'd like, a small set menu.

Do you take a deposit?

No deposit for tables up to 8. For private events or full restaurant buy-outs we may request a small deposit, refundable against the bill.

How long is a table held?

We hold reservations for 15 minutes past the booking time. If you're running late, just message us.

Can you accommodate allergies and dietary needs?

Yes — vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options are clearly tagged on the menu. Please note any allergies on the form and we'll flag your table.

Is the restaurant accessible?

The dining room is on street level. For specific accessibility needs (wheelchair, mobility), message us in advance so we can prepare the best table for you.
