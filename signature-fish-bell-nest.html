The Signature

The Wild Fish
& The Bell Nest.

Cookaki's signature Bell fish dish (€29.90): a wild Aegean fish plated with “the Bell Nest” — a marrow filled with trahanás simmered in fish broth, brightened with brined cherry tomatoes and a silky avgolémono sauce. Aegean fish, given a Greek soul.

What's on the plate

Half fish, half nest — one idea

The dish is built in two halves that belong together: the sea and the field.

  • Wild Aegean fish — filleted clean, cooked to just-set
  • The Bell Nest — a marrow (kolokythi) stuffed with trahanás simmered in a rich fish broth
  • Brined cherry tomatoes (armyrá domatíni) — a sharp, savoury counterpoint
  • Avgolémono — the classic Greek egg-and-lemon sauce, poured to finish

€29.90. With bread, water and a glass of wine, budget about €40–45 per person. Best enjoyed slowly.

The idea

Giving Aegean fish a Greek identity

Great fish speaks for itself — but our chef wanted it to speak Greek. The answer was trahanás: the old fermented-grain staple of the Greek countryside. Simmered in fish broth and cradled in a marrow, it turns a simple fillet into something rooted in place and memory — the brine, the grain, the lemon. It is the kind of plate that gives the kitchen “things to say”.

We're taking it further: because guests — Greek and foreign alike — keep asking about the trahanás, we're working with a small pasta workshop to produce our own house-made “Bell trahaná” from our own raw ingredients. Provenance you can taste, all the way down to the grain.

FAQ

The signature dish, answered

What is Cookaki's signature dish?
“The Wild Fish & The Bell Nest” (€29.90) — wild Aegean fish with a marrow of trahanás simmered in fish broth, brined cherry tomatoes and avgolémono. Petmeza 5, Koukáki, five minutes from the Acropolis Museum.
What is trahanás?
A traditional Greek grain — cracked wheat fermented with milk or yogurt, then dried. We simmer it in fish broth inside a marrow to make the Bell Nest, and are developing our own house-made Bell trahaná.
How much should I budget?
The dish is €29.90; with bread, water and a glass of wine, roughly €40–45 per person.
Is it always on?
It uses wild, seasonal fish, so availability follows the catch. Reserve and ask the team, or WhatsApp +30 698 237 3505 to check the day's fish.
