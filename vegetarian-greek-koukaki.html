Vegetarian & vegan

Plant-based Greek cooking,
done properly.

Greek food has always been half-vegetarian — built on olive oil, vegetables, pulses and the morning market. At Cookaki in Koukáki, that tradition isn't an afterthought menu; it's a daily dish cooked from scratch, five minutes from the Acropolis Museum.

The daily dish

Garden Dish of the Day

Each morning we cook a Garden Dish of the Day (€8.90) around whatever arrived from the local laïkí market — a seasonal vegetable stew or an oven-baked vegetable plate. Some days it's briám (roasted summer vegetables), some days gemistá (tomatoes and peppers stuffed with herbed rice), some days fasolákia (green beans braised in tomato and olive oil). It can usually be prepared vegan on request — just ask your server.

This is the heart of Greek home cooking: the ladera, dishes "of the oil," where vegetables are slow-cooked in good olive oil until sweet and silky. No imitation meat, no compromise — just the way Greek grandmothers have cooked vegetables for generations.

A note for visitors

Why Greece is easy for vegetarians

Because of the Orthodox calendar's many fasting periods, Greek cuisine developed an enormous repertoire of nistísima — dishes made without meat, dairy or eggs. That means a vegetarian or vegan traveller in Athens isn't choosing from a token corner of the menu; they're eating some of the most beloved dishes in the culture. At Cookaki we lean into that: the kitchen's vegetable cooking is treated with the same care as the moussaka it's been famous for since 1956. Coming from the Acropolis or exploring Koukáki, you'll eat well here whatever you do or don't eat.

FAQ

Vegetarian & vegan questions

Does Cookaki have vegetarian options?
Yes — a Garden Dish of the Day (€8.90) every day, built on the morning's market vegetables, plus Greek salad, sfakianí pita and several vegetarian mezédes.
Do you have vegan food?
Yes. Much of traditional Greek cooking is naturally vegan — ladera, gígantes beans, briám and many mezédes. The Garden Dish can usually be made vegan; just ask.
Are there gluten-free choices?
The Garden Dish and Greek salad are naturally gluten-free, and many dishes can be adapted. Tell your server about any allergy or intolerance.
How much does a vegetarian meal cost?
The Garden Dish is €8.90; a full vegetarian meal runs about €15 per person with a glass of wine.
